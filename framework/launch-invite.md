# Launch templates

## 1. Personal invite (send individually, by text or email)

Personal beats mass. Change the wording so it sounds like you, and send it one person at a time.

> Hey [Name],
>
> I'm starting something small: a short email every couple of weeks with what I'm thinking about, learning, and figuring out about life. Less social media, more like a letter.
>
> I'm only sharing it with people I'm close to for now, and I'd love for you to be one of them. No pressure at all. Want me to add you?
>
> [Your name]

Only add the people who say yes.

---

## 2. Welcome email (automatic, sent when someone subscribes)

**Subject:** You're in. Here's what to expect.
**Preview:** A quick hello, and one small favor.

> Hi there,
>
> Thank you for being here. It means more than you'd think.
>
> **What this is:** every other [Sunday], I'll send one idea I've been thinking about, a few things I'm learning, and a couple of things worth your time. It takes about five minutes to read.
>
> **Why I'm doing it:** [your one-sentence why].
>
> **One small favor:** hit reply and tell me *one thing you've been thinking about lately*. I read every reply, and it also helps these emails land in your inbox instead of spam.
>
> Talk soon,
> [Your name]

---

## 3. "Forward this" line (add to the footer from Stage 2)

> *Know someone who'd enjoy this? Forward it to them. And if this was forwarded to you, you can subscribe here: [link].*
